📖 Chapter Overview

Democracy is a form of government in which people participate in choosing their representatives and influencing how they are governed.

This chapter introduces the meaning, features and importance of democracy.

🗳️ What is Democracy?

The word democracy comes from the Greek words demos, meaning people, and kratos, meaning rule or power.

Democracy therefore broadly means government by the people.

⭐ Key Features of Democracy

  • People have a role in choosing their representatives.
  • Elections are held regularly.
  • Citizens have political rights.
  • The rule of law applies to everyone.
  • Government is accountable to the people.
  • Citizens can express their views.

🗳️ Elections

Elections allow citizens to choose representatives who make decisions on their behalf.

Free and fair elections are an important part of a functioning democracy.

👥 People's Participation

Democracy is not limited to voting. Citizens can participate in public life in many ways.

  • Voting in elections
  • Discussing public issues
  • Expressing opinions
  • Participating in community activities
  • Holding representatives accountable

⚖️ Rule of Law

The rule of law means that laws apply to all people, including those who hold positions of power.

No individual or institution should be above the law.

🧑‍⚖️ Rights and Equality

Democracy is closely connected with fundamental rights and equality before the law.

Citizens should be treated with dignity and should have opportunities to participate in public life.

🗣️ Freedom of Expression

Citizens in a democracy should be able to express their views and discuss public matters within the framework of the law.

A healthy democracy allows different viewpoints to be discussed peacefully.

🏛️ Institutions

Democratic governments work through institutions. These institutions help make decisions, implement laws and hold power accountable.

  • Legislature
  • Executive
  • Judiciary
  • Election institutions
  • Local government institutions

🇮🇳 Democracy in India

India is a democratic republic. The Constitution provides the framework for democratic government and establishes important rights and institutions.

Citizens elect representatives at different levels of government.

🏘️ Local Democracy

Democracy also operates at the local level through institutions such as Panchayats and Municipalities.

Local government allows people to participate in decisions concerning their communities.

🤝 Importance of Democracy

  • Encourages people's participation.
  • Provides peaceful ways to change governments.
  • Promotes accountability.
  • Protects political participation.
  • Allows different viewpoints.
  • Supports equality before the law.

⚠️ Challenges to Democracy

Democracy requires active and responsible participation. Problems such as inequality, misinformation, discrimination and lack of participation can weaken democratic processes.

Citizens, institutions and governments all have roles in strengthening democracy.

⭐ Quick Revision

  • Democracy means government by the people.
  • Elections are an important part of democracy.
  • Citizens participate in public life.
  • The rule of law applies to everyone.
  • Democracy supports political equality.
  • Freedom of expression allows different views to be discussed.
  • Democratic institutions help exercise and control power.
  • India is a democratic republic.
  • Local governments provide opportunities for participation at the local level.
