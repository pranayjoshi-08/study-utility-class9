📐 Measuring Space: Perimeter and Area

Class 9 Mathematics — Ganita Manjari

📖 Chapter Overview

In this chapter, we learn how to measure the boundary and area of different plane figures. We explore perimeter, circumference, area, circles, triangles, sectors and other geometrical shapes.

📏 Perimeter

The perimeter of a shape is the total length of its boundary.

Important Formulas

  • Square = 4 × side
  • Rectangle = 2 × (length + breadth)
  • Equilateral triangle = 3 × side

⭕ Circumference of a Circle

The perimeter of a circle is called its circumference.

C = 2πr

Since diameter d = 2r:

C = πd

Here, r is the radius and d is the diameter.

🔵 The Constant π

The ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter is constant. This constant is represented by π.

For calculations, π is often taken as:

π ≈ 22/7

🌙 Arc Length

An arc is a part of the circumference of a circle.

If an arc subtends an angle θ at the centre, its length is:

Arc length = (θ/360°) × 2πr

📐 Area of Plane Figures

Rectangle

Area = length × breadth

Square

Area = side²

Parallelogram

Area = base × height

Triangle

Area = 1/2 × base × height

Trapezium

Area = 1/2 × (sum of parallel sides) × height

🔺 Heron's Formula

Heron's formula can be used to find the area of a triangle when all three sides are known.

For sides a, b and c:

s = (a + b + c) / 2

where s is the semi-perimeter.

Then:

Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]

🔷 Brahmagupta's Formula

For a cyclic quadrilateral with sides a, b, c and d, let

s = (a + b + c + d) / 2

Then its area is:

Area = √[(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)(s − d)]

⭕ Area of a Circle

The area enclosed by a circle is:

Area = πr²

where r is the radius.

🥧 Area of a Sector

A sector is the region enclosed by two radii and an arc.

For a sector with central angle θ:

Area = (θ/360°) × πr²

✏️ Solved Examples

Example 1

Find the circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm.

Show Solution

C = 2πr

= 2 × 22/7 × 7

= 44 cm

✅ Answer = 44 cm

Example 2

Find the area of a rectangle of length 12 cm and breadth 5 cm.

Show Solution

Area = length × breadth

= 12 × 5

✅ Answer = 60 cm²

Example 3

Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm.

Show Solution

Area = πr²

= 22/7 × 7 × 7

= 154 cm²

✅ Answer = 154 cm²

🧠 Important Formulas

  • Square perimeter = 4a
  • Rectangle perimeter = 2(l + b)
  • Circle circumference = 2πr
  • Rectangle area = lb
  • Square area = a²
  • Triangle area = 1/2 × base × height
  • Circle area = πr²
  • Sector area = (θ/360°) × πr²
  • Heron's formula = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]
