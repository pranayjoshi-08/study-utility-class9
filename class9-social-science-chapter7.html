📖 Chapter Overview

Elections are an essential part of a democracy. They allow citizens to choose their representatives and participate in the process of government.

This chapter explains electoral systems, representation, political parties, electoral rolls and the role of the Election Commission of India.

🗳️ Why Do Elections Matter?

In a representative democracy, people cannot directly make every government decision. They therefore elect representatives who act on their behalf.

  • People choose representatives.
  • Citizens can change their representatives.
  • Elected governments remain accountable to voters.
  • Elections provide a peaceful way to transfer political power.

👥 Direct and Indirect Elections

Direct Elections

In a direct election, voters directly choose their representatives.

Indirect Elections

In an indirect election, elected representatives or members of an electoral body choose another representative.

🇮🇳 Elections in India

India follows a system of representative democracy. Citizens vote to elect representatives at different levels of government.

Elections are conducted according to constitutional and legal provisions.

🗳️ First-Past-the-Post System

In the First-Past-the-Post system, the candidate who receives the highest number of votes in a constituency is elected.

A candidate does not necessarily need more than half of all votes to win.

📊 Proportional Representation

Proportional Representation aims to make the number of representatives reflect the share of votes received by parties or candidates.

India uses different electoral systems for different offices and institutions.

🔄 Single Transferable Vote

The Single Transferable Vote system allows voters to rank candidates according to preference.

It is used for certain elections in India, including elections to some constitutional and legislative offices.

🗺️ Constituencies

A constituency is an area whose voters elect a representative.

Constituencies help organise elections and representation across the country.

📏 Delimitation

Delimitation refers to the process of determining or redrawing the boundaries of electoral constituencies.

Its purpose includes creating constituencies for electoral representation according to the applicable legal framework.

📋 Electoral Rolls

An electoral roll is the official list of eligible voters for an electoral area.

Maintaining accurate electoral rolls is important so that eligible citizens can participate in elections.

🏛️ Election Commission of India

The Election Commission of India is the constitutional authority responsible for conducting elections to Parliament, State Legislatures and the offices of the President and Vice-President.

  • Supervises elections.
  • Prepares and updates electoral rolls.
  • Conducts elections according to law.
  • Works to ensure free and fair elections.

📋 Electoral Roll Revision

Electoral rolls need to be updated regularly so that eligible voters are included and ineligible entries are removed according to the applicable rules.

Accurate electoral rolls strengthen the electoral process.

🏛️ Political Parties

Political parties bring together people who share political ideas and seek to influence or form government.

Parties nominate candidates, present policies and participate in elections.

🤝 Representation

Elections provide a mechanism through which citizens are represented in legislative bodies.

Representation is important because different regions and communities need a voice in government.

⚖️ Free and Fair Elections

For elections to be meaningful, voters must be able to make their choices freely and the electoral process must follow established laws and procedures.

  • Accurate electoral rolls
  • Clear electoral rules
  • Secret voting
  • Independent election administration
  • Fair opportunity for candidates and parties

⭐ Quick Revision

  • Elections allow citizens to choose representatives.
  • India uses representative democracy.
  • Direct and indirect elections are different methods of choosing representatives.
  • India uses the First-Past-the-Post system for many direct elections.
  • Proportional Representation is used for certain elections.
  • Constituencies organise electoral representation.
  • Delimitation deals with constituency boundaries.
  • Electoral rolls contain the names of eligible voters.
  • The Election Commission of India conducts important elections.
  • Free and fair elections are essential to democracy.
