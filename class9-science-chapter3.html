📖 Chapter Overview

In multicellular organisms, cells with similar structures and functions work together as tissues.

Different tissues perform specialised functions and help an organism carry out its life processes efficiently.

🌿 Plant Tissues

Plant tissues can be broadly grouped according to their structure and function.

Meristematic Tissue

Meristematic tissues contain actively dividing cells. They are responsible for the growth of plants.

  • Apical meristem - helps increase length.
  • Lateral meristem - helps increase thickness.
  • Intercalary meristem - helps growth in certain regions.

🌳 Permanent Tissues

Permanent tissues are formed from cells that have differentiated and generally lost the ability to divide.

Parenchyma

Parenchyma cells commonly perform storage and other functions in plants.

Collenchyma

Collenchyma provides support and flexibility to growing plant parts.

Sclerenchyma

Sclerenchyma provides mechanical strength to plant parts.

💧 Conducting Tissues

Xylem

Xylem is mainly involved in transporting water and dissolved minerals from the roots to other parts of the plant.

Phloem

Phloem transports food and other organic substances from the parts where they are produced to other parts of the plant.

🐾 Animal Tissues

Animal tissues are specialised for different functions such as protection, support, movement and communication.

Major Types

  • 🧱 Epithelial tissue
  • 🔗 Connective tissue
  • 💪 Muscular tissue
  • 🧠 Nervous tissue

🧱 Epithelial Tissue

Epithelial tissue forms protective coverings and linings of many parts of the body.

It can also perform functions such as absorption and secretion depending on its location.

🔗 Connective Tissue

Connective tissues connect, support or protect different parts of the body.

Examples

  • Blood
  • Bone
  • Cartilage
  • Adipose tissue
  • Tendons and ligaments

🩸 Blood

Blood is a connective tissue that transports substances throughout the body.

Its components include plasma, red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets.

Component Main Role
Plasma Transports dissolved substances
Red blood cells Transport oxygen
White blood cells Help defend the body
Platelets Help in blood clotting

💪 Muscular Tissue

Muscular tissue is specialised for contraction and helps produce movement.

Types

  • Skeletal muscle
  • Smooth muscle
  • Cardiac muscle

🧠 Nervous Tissue

Nervous tissue receives and transmits information through electrical and chemical signals.

The basic functional cells of nervous tissue are neurons.
