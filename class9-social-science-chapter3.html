📖 Chapter Overview

The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth. It plays an important role in supporting life and controlling weather and climate.

In this chapter, we learn about the composition and structure of the atmosphere, weather, climate, seasons, monsoon and climate change.

🌍 What is the Atmosphere?

The atmosphere is the envelope of gases surrounding Earth. It is held around the planet by gravity.

The atmosphere provides gases necessary for life and influences Earth's temperature and weather.

🧪 Composition of the Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere is mainly made up of nitrogen and oxygen, along with smaller amounts of other gases.

Gas Approximate proportion
Nitrogen 78%
Oxygen 21%
Other gases About 1%

☁️ Layers of the Atmosphere

The atmosphere is divided into different layers based mainly on changes in temperature with altitude.

  • Troposphere – lowest layer; most weather occurs here.
  • Stratosphere – contains the ozone layer.
  • Mesosphere – a very cold atmospheric layer.
  • Thermosphere – temperatures can become very high.
  • Exosphere – outermost region of the atmosphere.

🌡️ Weather

Weather refers to the short-term condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.

Elements of Weather

  • Temperature
  • Air pressure
  • Humidity
  • Wind
  • Cloud cover
  • Precipitation

🌎 Climate

Climate refers to the long-term pattern of weather conditions of a place.

Climate is studied over a long period and helps us understand typical conditions of different regions.

⚖️ Weather vs Climate

Weather Climate
Short-term atmospheric condition Long-term pattern of atmospheric conditions
Can change quickly Changes over longer periods
Example: Today's temperature Example: Typical rainfall pattern of a region

☀️ Factors Affecting Climate

Climate differs from place to place because of several geographical factors.

  • Latitude
  • Altitude
  • Distance from the sea
  • Relief and mountains
  • Winds
  • Ocean currents

🇮🇳 Climate of India

India's climate is influenced by its large size, location, mountains, surrounding seas and seasonal wind systems.

The Indian monsoon plays a major role in the climate and agricultural life of the country.

🌧️ Monsoon

Monsoon refers to the seasonal reversal of winds. In India, the monsoon brings most of the country's annual rainfall.

The southwest monsoon is especially important for agriculture, water resources and everyday life.

🌦️ Seasons in India

India experiences distinct seasonal patterns associated with changes in temperature, winds and rainfall.

  • ❄️ Winter season
  • ☀️ Summer or pre-monsoon season
  • 🌧️ Monsoon or rainy season
  • 🍂 Post-monsoon or retreating monsoon season

🌱 Climate and Human Life

Climate influences agriculture, water availability, vegetation, clothing, housing and many human activities.

People adapt their lifestyles and economic activities according to local climatic conditions.

🌡️ Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's climate patterns.

Human activities that increase greenhouse gas emissions contribute significantly to current climate change.

🏭 Greenhouse Effect

Certain gases in the atmosphere trap some of the heat radiated by Earth. This natural process is called the greenhouse effect.

An increase in greenhouse gases can strengthen this warming effect and contribute to global warming.

🌳 What Can We Do?

  • Use energy efficiently.
  • Reduce unnecessary consumption.
  • Protect forests and plant trees.
  • Use cleaner sources of energy.
  • Reduce waste and pollution.
  • Use public transport or shared transport when possible.

⭐ Quick Revision

  • The atmosphere is the gaseous envelope around Earth.
  • Nitrogen and oxygen make up most of the atmosphere.
  • The troposphere is the lowest atmospheric layer.
  • Most weather occurs in the troposphere.
  • The stratosphere contains the ozone layer.
  • Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions.
  • Climate describes long-term patterns of weather.
  • Monsoon winds are seasonally reversing winds.
  • India's climate is strongly influenced by the monsoon.
  • Human activities can contribute to climate change.
