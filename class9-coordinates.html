📖 Chapter Overview

Coordinates help us describe the exact position of a point on a plane. They are widely used in maps, graphs, navigation and mathematics.

📌 What are Coordinates?

The position of a point can be represented using an ordered pair of numbers written as (x, y).

The first number represents the position along the horizontal direction and the second number represents the position along the vertical direction.

Example: (3, 5)

Here, x = 3 and y = 5.

📐 Coordinate Plane

A coordinate plane is formed by two perpendicular number lines:

  • X-axis — horizontal axis
  • Y-axis — vertical axis

The point where the two axes meet is called the origin.

The coordinates of the origin are: (0, 0)

🧭 Four Quadrants

The coordinate plane is divided into four regions called quadrants.

  • Quadrant I → (+, +)
  • Quadrant II → (-, +)
  • Quadrant III → (-, -)
  • Quadrant IV → (+, -)

⭐ Important Points

  • The x-coordinate is written first.
  • The y-coordinate is written second.
  • The origin is (0, 0).
  • Points on the x-axis have y = 0.
  • Points on the y-axis have x = 0.

✏️ Solved Examples

Example 1

Show Answer

Example 2

Show Answer

Example 3

Show Answer
