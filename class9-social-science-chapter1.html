📖 Chapter Overview

Social Science helps us understand people, society, places, institutions, economies and the changes that take place around us.

It connects different areas of knowledge to help us understand India and the wider world.

🌍 What is Social Science?

Social Science is the systematic study of human society and the relationships between people and their surroundings.

It helps us understand how people live, work, organise themselves and make decisions.

📚 Major Areas of Social Science

Area What it studies
🏺 History Past events, societies and changes over time
🌎 Geography Earth, places, people and their environment
🏛️ Political Science Government, institutions, citizenship and power
💰 Economics Production, distribution, consumption and resources

🏺 History

History studies the human past. It helps us understand how societies changed and how events shaped the present.

Sources of History

  • Inscriptions
  • Coins
  • Manuscripts
  • Buildings and monuments
  • Archaeological remains
  • Oral traditions

🌎 Geography

Geography studies places, Earth's physical features, people and the relationship between humans and their environment.

Important Areas

  • Landforms
  • Climate
  • Rivers and water
  • Natural resources
  • Population
  • Human settlements

🏛️ Political Science

Political Science helps us understand governments, political institutions, rights, duties and the ways people participate in public life.

  • Government
  • Constitution
  • Citizenship
  • Rights and duties
  • Democracy
  • Political institutions

💰 Economics

Economics studies how people and societies use limited resources to satisfy their needs and wants.

  • Resources
  • Production
  • Consumption
  • Markets
  • Employment
  • Economic development

🔗 How the Subjects are Connected

History, Geography, Political Science and Economics are closely connected.

For example, geography can influence where people settle, history explains how societies change, political science examines how they are governed, and economics studies how people use resources.

🔍 Asking Questions in Social Science

Social Science encourages us to ask questions, examine evidence and develop explanations.

  • What happened?
  • Why did it happen?
  • Where did it happen?
  • Who was affected?
  • What changed?
  • What evidence supports the explanation?

🧠 Evidence and Reasoning

Good Social Science learning is based on evidence. We should examine information carefully instead of accepting every claim without questioning it.

Comparing different sources can help us form a more reliable understanding of an event or issue.
